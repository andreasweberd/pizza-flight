==============================================================================
TASK 06 - SQL AGGREGATIONS & GROUPING (COUNT, SUM, AVG, MIN, MAX, GROUP BY)
==============================================================================

OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Answer real business analytics questions for Pizza Flight using aggregate
functions, GROUP BY, HAVING, and joins.


EXERCISES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Write one SQL query for each of the following 10 questions in solutions.sql:

1. Total number of orders:
   Count the total number of rows in the orders table.

2. Completed revenue:
   Calculate the total revenue (SUM(total_price)) of all COMPLETED orders.

3. Pizza price statistics:
   Find the minimum (MIN), maximum (MAX), and average (AVG, rounded to 2
   decimal places with ROUND(..., 2)) base_price of all pizzas.

4. Pizzas per category (GROUP BY):
   Count how many pizzas exist in each category.
   Show: category, pizza_count, ordered by pizza_count descending.

5. Orders per payment method:
   For each payment_method, show how many COMPLETED orders were paid that
   way and the total revenue (SUM(total_price)).

6. Revenue per vending machine:
   Join machines and orders to calculate total revenue (SUM(total_price))
   per machine for COMPLETED orders only.
   Show: code, city, total_revenue, ordered by total_revenue descending.

7. All machines including those with zero orders (LEFT JOIN):
   List EVERY vending machine (code, city) and its number of orders
   (COUNT(o.order_id)). Machines without orders must appear with 0.

8. Most popular pizzas:
   Join pizzas, order_items, and orders to find the total quantity sold for
   each pizza across COMPLETED orders.
   Show: name, total_sold, ordered by total_sold descending.

9. Frequent customers (HAVING):
   Find all registered customers who have placed AT LEAST 2 COMPLETED orders.
   Show: first_name, last_name, order_count.

10. Low total stock per machine (HAVING):
    Using machine_inventory and machines, calculate the total number of
    pizzas currently in stock (SUM(quantity)) for each machine, and show
    only machines with FEWER THAN 25 pizzas in stock.
    Show: code, city, total_stock.
